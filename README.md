An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run".

`main.py` writes a two-line explanation of a swing to a 10-year-old — one vivid analogy, then one honest sentence about what the analogy maps and what it leaves out — to `/output/entry.txt` (stdlib only, deterministic).

## Entry

> A swing is a chair tied to the sky that lets you borrow the wind for a few seconds.
>
> It maps the flying feeling and the going back and forth, but it leaves out the chains and the push — a swing can't move without someone's legs or hands.
